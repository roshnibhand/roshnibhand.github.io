Project

Event Workflow Reflection Board

A workflow analysis exercise for mapping handoffs, decision points, and operational friction that affect customer experience and execution quality.

April 09, 2026 • 4 min read
operations analyticsworkflow designprocess improvement

Context

Complex event-tech work depends on clean handoffs between people, tools, and timelines. Even small gaps in context can create rework, confusion, or a weaker customer experience.

What I mapped

  • Where requests move between teams, tools, and timelines
  • Where missing context creates delays, follow-up work, or execution risk
  • Which moments have the biggest impact on customer experience and internal efficiency

Why it matters

This exercise helps me think like an analyst about processes, not just outputs. It sharpens how I identify failure points, ask better questions, and recommend improvements.

Next step

I want to turn this into a more formal framework for process reviews, workflow documentation, and service-quality analysis.

Why This Matters

This project reflects the kind of role I am targeting: work that requires clear problem framing, analytical depth, and communication that helps teams act.
