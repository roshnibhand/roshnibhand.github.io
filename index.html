Outside Work

Interests that sharpen perspective beyond the dashboard

Travel, cooking, and singing are the interests that most often bring me back to perspective, calm, and the simple joy of paying attention to life.

Travel

Travel gives me perspective in a very real way. I love noticing culture, sitting quietly in natural places, listening to a river flow, looking at the mountains, and even just staring at the road during a road trip. It reminds me how much meaning there is in slowing down and really taking in where you are.

Cooking

Cooking feels like meditation for me. I love playing music while I cook, and that combination instantly calms me down. It has also taught me a life lesson I carry everywhere: even when a recipe starts going wrong, you can still turn it into something right if you stay present, trust yourself, and do not panic.

Singing

Singing helps me reset my mood and return to myself. I love the artistic side of it too, because getting the notes right is not just about voice, it is about listening properly. It reminds me that expression and attention go together. Follow more of my singing on Roshnibhandula (@roshnibhandula26).
